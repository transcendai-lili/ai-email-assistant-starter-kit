# AI Email Assistant Starter Kit

[Download the complete starter kit](https://github.com/transcendai-lili/ai-email-assistant-starter-kit/releases/latest/download/AI-Email-Assistant.zip)

A free Transcend AI kit for setting up a ChatGPT project that drafts email replies in your own voice using your approved business information.

## Start here

Download and extract `AI-Email-Assistant.zip`, then open `SET_UP_INSTRUCTION.pdf` and follow the steps in order.

The download contains six files:

- `SET_UP_INSTRUCTION.pdf`: the step-by-step setup guide.
- `Communication-Style-Skill.md`: a template for your preferences and approved writing examples.
- `Humanizer-Skill.md`: instructions for a final language check.
- `SAMPLE-Transcend AI - Capability Statement - Client XYZ.pdf`.
- `SAMPLE-Transcend AI - Price Guide.pdf`.
- `SAMPLE-Transcend AI - Fictional Client Enquiry.docx`.

The three SAMPLE files show what to prepare. Set up your assistant with your own approved service and pricing documents, as the guide explains.

You paste an enquiry into ChatGPT, check the draft, then copy it into your email app and send it yourself. The starter kit does not connect to or monitor your inbox.
